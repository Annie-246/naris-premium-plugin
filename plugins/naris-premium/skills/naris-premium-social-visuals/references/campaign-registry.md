# Naris Premium campaign registry

This registry is the routing source for Campaign mode. A campaign may be used only when its dedicated sub-skill is listed here and available in the personal skill directory.

## Registered campaigns

### Hoa Hậu Việt Nam 2026

- Display name: `Hoa Hậu Việt Nam 2026`
- Aliases: `Hoa Hậu Việt Nam`, `HHVN`, `HHVN 2026`
- Slug: `hoa-hau-viet-nam`
- Skill: `naris-premium-campaign-hoa-hau-viet-nam`
- Status: `active`
- Key visual: warm textured paper, pageant pink lotus line art and brush accents, warm gold editorial serif, with Naris on the left and HHVN on the right.

When a campaign is created, add one entry with:

- campaign display name;
- accepted aliases;
- campaign slug;
- exact skill name: `naris-premium-campaign-<campaign-slug>`;
- status: `active` or `archived`;
- optional effective dates;
- one-line description of the key visual.

Do not reuse an archived campaign for new work unless the user explicitly asks for it. Do not route by approximate visual similarity; match the name or alias.
